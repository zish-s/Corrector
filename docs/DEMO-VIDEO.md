# 60-second demo video script

**Voiceover (one paragraph):**

“This is Corrector, a Socratic DSA coach that lives beside your code. I’m solving Longest
Substring, and I’m stuck. I press `Ctrl+Shift+Y`; Corrector reads the live editor, finds the
exact line where `MaxLen` is missing its type, and instead of giving me the fix, a red-pen
note appears beside it: *‘What type should MaxLen be, and why?’* — with a small animated
window showing the idea. I can say *I don’t know* to move one rung smaller, or *Just tell
me* to climb the hint ladder. Every question is checked before it appears: one question,
no early code block, no repeated question, and no full solution unless I explicitly ask.
On a site without a readable editor, I capture the screen and Gemma 4 extracts the
problem, code, language, and anchor line as structured JSON — the tutoring decision stays
local. The static verdict tells me whether my approach looks optimal or brute force, and
the per-topic profile adjusts to my level. The key stays on the backend, never in the
extension. Corrector doesn’t just give answers — it makes me find them.”
